# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

* Six-case noun declension (plain declension) driven by vowel harmony and
  suffix-initial consonant assimilation.
* `Word` metadata: proper nouns, abbreviations, pronunciation override.
* `inflect_detailed` with `Uncertainty` diagnostics and `strict` mode.
* Immutable `Lexicon` with the irregular personal pronouns мен, сен, ал.
